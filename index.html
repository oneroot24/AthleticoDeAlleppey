02 — A landscape unlike any other

Where the land
meets the water.

Kuttanad is one of India’s most extraordinary landscapes: a living network of canals, backwaters and paddy fields reclaimed from the water. Here, life moves with the rhythm of the rivers — and every kilometre carries a sense of place.

This is more than a race route. It’s an invitation to experience the heritage, resilience and quiet beauty of Alappuzha on foot.

A few useful details

Before you begin.

Who can participate?

Participants must be 18 years or older on race day, 10 January 2027. This applies to all four race categories, including the 5 km Fun Run.

When does registration close?

Registration is scheduled to close on 3 January 2027. Please register early and check the official site for category availability.

Can I register a running club or group?

The registration system is intended to support group registration through a club coordinator. Group checkout and club access will depend on the final registration platform being enabled.

How will I receive my bib number?

Once payment is successfully confirmed by the registration system, participants should receive a confirmation with their allocated bib number and race-kit collection details by email and SMS.

Where can I collect my race kit?

Race-kit collection details, including venue and timings, will be shared with confirmed participants by the organiser before race day.
